# Manufacturer logos: sources

Every logo here was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, where each file is marked
public domain (simple text and shapes, below the threshold of originality). They
are still **trademarks of their owners**. The app uses them only to identify the
maker of a connected display. Do not use them to suggest that a manufacturer
endorses or is affiliated with Neolink, and check with counsel before shipping
them in a commercial product.

The originals are in `original/`. The files beside this one were produced from
them by `tools/build_brand_logos.py` (cleaned, cropped, single color).
